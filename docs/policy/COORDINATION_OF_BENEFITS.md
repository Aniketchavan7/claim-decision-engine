# Policy Specification: Coordination of Benefits (COB) Multi-Payer Rules

## 1. Order of Determination
When a claimant is covered under multiple active health policies:
1. **Primary Plan**: The policy covering the claimant as an employee or primary subscriber pays first without reference to other coverages.
2. **Secondary Plan**: The policy covering the claimant as a dependent or secondary top-up policy applies deductible calculations solely against the remaining out-of-pocket balance.
